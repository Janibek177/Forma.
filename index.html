Materials

01 / CLAY

Portuguese Stoneware

Formed from rich natural deposits, offering unmatched tactile warmth and long-term durability.

02 / GLAZE

Non-Toxic Pigments

Formulated in-house using organic earth minerals, yielding muted natural tones.

03 / FIRE

High-Temp Kiln

Fired twice at over 1,250°C to vitrify the stoneware, making every piece food-safe.

The Process

Hand Throwing

Raw clay is balanced and shaped on the wheel.

Air Drying

Pieces rest for days in climate-controlled rooms.

Dip Glazing

Vessels are hand-dipped in custom mineral glazes.

Final Firing

Intense heat locks the glaze into a resilient finish.
